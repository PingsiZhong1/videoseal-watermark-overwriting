Slot-based multi-watermark training prototype

Design
- The 96-bit payload was divided into three ordered 32-bit slots.
- The training objective used sequential states A, A+B, and A+B+C.
- The final decoder was expected to recover all active slots directly.

Environment
- Apple M2 Pro
- MPS device
- Official VideoSeal v0.0 checkpoint
- One official example video
- 20 training steps

Implementation note
- The default mixed mask module caused inconsistent Tensor/NumPy errors.
- It was replaced with NoMaskEmbedder for the local smoke-training prototype.

Initial result
- The training path, backpropagation, and checkpoint saving ran successfully.
- The 20-step single-video run did not stably recover all active slots.
- This is an implementation prototype, not evidence that multi-watermark coexistence has been solved.

Next requirement
- Multi-video data, longer training, and explicit loss terms that protect earlier slots.
